2026 is our 26th year of an animated Christmas lights display. The show opens on Tuesday, November 24, 2026 and runs nightly through Friday, January 1, 2027.

Outdoor construction started November 6 and wrapped up after 11 days of work. Time-lapse videos of the first 10 days are in the Construction section below.

We're adding four new songs this year, bringing the show to 33 songs and 88 minutes of music: Run Run Rudolph, Carol of the Bells (Epic Version), [Shake the Snow Globe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ID9gpeOFGiQ), and [A Smoky Mountain Christmas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BPBax2Ohb8E), which we added in remembrance of Dolly Parton. The 75,805 lights and the rest of the hardware carry over from [2025](/christmas/2025/).

We also added a tribute to Fiona, the Cincinnati Zoo's beloved hippo, at the end of [I Want a Hippopotamus for Christmas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ymw7B1Vkwao). Images of Fiona have been part of that sequence since we first added the song in 2017, so honoring her there felt only fitting.

New behind the scenes this year is a [car counter](/technology/car-counter/). Two cameras and AI object detection estimate how many cars are parked out front watching the show. It helps us understand attendance and traffic patterns, and it lets you check how busy we are before you head over. Our servers also grew to four virtual machines: three running our home Kubernetes cluster and one in the cloud hosting our public websites.

Make your plans now to join us! One of the most special nights to visit is Christmas Eve, when neighbors gather out front to count down the final seconds as the [countdown clock](/technology/clock/) hits zero at midnight. Check the [visit page](/christmas/visit/) for show times, directions, and the radio station.
